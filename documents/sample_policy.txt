Employee Handbook Excerpt: Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per calendar year, credited at
1.25 days per month. PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or illness.

Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year;
any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

To request PTO, submit a request through the HR portal at least 5 business days
in advance for planned absences. For unplanned absences (e.g. illness), notify
your manager as soon as possible on the day of absence.

New employees begin accruing PTO from their start date but cannot use accrued
PTO until they have completed 90 days of employment.
